Your Web Research and Learning Assistant

The purpose of StudyGuard is to help students understand complex academic content, save and organize research materials, and stay safe online while conducting web-based research.

Purpose of StudyGuard

StudyGuard is a browser extension designed specifically for students who need to understand difficult academic terms, save important information from webpages, organize their research, and identify potentially unsafe links. StudyGuard works entirely within your browser and provides four essential tools for academic success.

📖 Understand Academic Content

  • Select any text on a webpage
  • Get simple explanations of complex terms
  • Access built-in dictionary definitions
  • Learn subject-specific concepts

📌 Save Research Materials

  • Clip important webpage content
  • Add notes and tags to clips
  • Capture source URLs automatically
  • Store videos, audio, and notes

📚 Organize Study Projects

  • Create research projects by subject
  • Organize clips into collections
  • Search and filter saved content
  • Manage all study resources

🛡️ Stay Safe Online

  • Analyze URLs for suspicious patterns
  • Detect phishing indicators
  • Identify potentially unsafe links
  • Get evidence-based risk assessments
