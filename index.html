StudyGuard

Your Web Research and Learning Assistant

StudyGuard is a browser extension designed for students to understand complex academic terms, save important research content, organize study projects, and stay safe online with rule-based URL security analysis.

What StudyGuard Does

StudyGuard helps students with web-based research by providing four essential tools in one extension.

📖 Understand Content

  • Select any text on a webpage
  • Get simple explanations of complex terms
  • Access built-in dictionary
  • Learn subject-specific concepts

📌 Save Research

  • Clip important webpage content
  • Add notes and tags
  • Capture source URLs automatically
  • Store videos, audio, and notes

📚 Organize Studies

  • Create research projects
  • Organize clips by subject
  • Search and filter saved content
  • Manage study resources

🛡️ Stay Safe Online

  • Analyze URLs for suspicious patterns
  • Detect phishing indicators
  • Identify potentially unsafe links
  • Get risk-based recommendations
