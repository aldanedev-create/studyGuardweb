Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2024

Overview

StudyGuard is a browser extension designed to help students with web research and learning. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our extension works.

Key Point: StudyGuard operates primarily on your device. Data is stored locally unless you choose to connect Google Drive for cloud storage.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Browsing history
  • Search queries
  • Passwords or credentials
  • Financial information
  • Location data

Data Stored Locally

By default, all StudyGuard data is stored locally on your device:

  • Saved clips: Text selections you choose to save
  • Research projects: Projects you create
  • Notes: Study notes you write
  • Settings: Your extension preferences
  • URL check history: URLs you manually check

Google Drive Integration (Optional)

Optional Feature: Google Drive integration is completely optional. You must explicitly connect your Google account to use this feature.

If you choose to connect Google Drive, StudyGuard may store:

  • Videos: Lecture recordings, tutorials
  • Audio files: Podcasts, voice notes
  • Notes: Study notes and documents
  • Clips: Your saved research clips

Google Drive Permissions

When connecting Google Drive, StudyGuard requests:

  • View and manage files in your Google Drive
  • Create folders for StudyGuard data
  • Upload/download StudyGuard files only

What StudyGuard Does NOT Access

  • Other files in your Google Drive
  • Your Google account information
  • Your emails or contacts
  • Files outside the StudyGuard folder

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. Data stored in Google Drive remains under your Google account's privacy settings.

Data Retention

Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall the extension. Google Drive data remains until you delete it from your Drive.

Third-Party Services

Google Drive and dictionary api is the third-party service used, and only if you opt in. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Security

  • Google Drive uses Google's security infrastructure
  • Files are encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • You control access through your Google account
  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time

Children's Privacy

StudyGuard does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
